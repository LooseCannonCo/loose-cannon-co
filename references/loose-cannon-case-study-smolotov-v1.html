The hot mess, decoded

27 likes. Zero paid history. One ghost town.

Smolotov has a product that slaps and a cultural lane wide open. Satirical poop bags. A loyal cult. Real demand. What they didn't have: a system to unlock any of it. No paid media history. Repeated Meta denials. No retargeting. No conversion path.

The brief was simple and unglamorous: survive Meta's approval gauntlet first, then build a paid acquisition engine from scratch. Then make organic and paid talk to each other. Then scale only when the data said go.

No clever hooks. No category-leading manifesto. Just a system that actually works, dressed in a brand that didn't ask permission.

01

Fix the Meta problem first.

Repositioned the product as a satirical gag gift, removed any references to real people, aligned every line of ad copy with Meta policy. Account approved. Path cleared.

From denied → approved in 3 weeks.

02

Launch intentionally. Scale on signal.

First Meta campaign live in January. Retargeting pools built across FB and IG. Spend scaled only when efficiency held. Cold spend stayed disciplined; warm spend did the heavy lifting.

3.40× blended ROAS. $10 CPA.

03

Build the funnel around the ads.

Paid doesn't live in a vacuum. Organic social fed trust signals. Email kept warm traffic returning. Shopify data fed back into audience targeting. One closed loop.

+460% revenue growth, Feb → May.
